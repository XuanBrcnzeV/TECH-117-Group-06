Traffic Light Warning System

An intelligent real-time alert system designed to prevent red-light running, reduce intersection collisions, and assist drivers in low-visibility conditions.

1. Introduction

Intersections remain one of the most hazardous locations on roadways due to sudden signal changes, driver distraction, and poor weather visibility. Our Traffic Light Warning System aims to bridge the communication gap between traffic infrastructure and drivers by providing timely visual and audio alerts prior to approaching a red or changing signal.

🎯 Project Goals & Success Criteria

Primary Goals:

  • Detect traffic light states (Green, Yellow, Red) accurately and transmit status updates in real time.
  • Provide drivers with clear, non-distracting visual and auditory warnings at a safe braking distance.
  • Minimize human reaction delays during sudden yellow-to-red transitions or fog/rain conditions.

Success Criteria (How we measure success):

  • Detection Accuracy: Achieve ≥ 95% accuracy in identifying signal states during prototype testing.
  • System Latency: Trigger driver alerts within < 0.5 seconds of a traffic signal state change.
  • Usability: Positive feedback from at least 85% of test users regarding alert clarity and timing.

⚠️ Constraints & Risks

Key Project Constraints:

  • Time & Schedule: Strict semester deadlines limit full-scale hardware deployment on actual roadways.
  • Budget & Hardware: Limited budget restricts the use of industrial-grade V2X (Vehicle-to-Everything) transceivers.
  • Technical Scope: Team members have varying experience levels in hardware-software integration.

Identified Risks & Backup Plans:

  • Risk 1 (Sensor/Camera Glare): Sunlight or night glare may affect optical signal detection.
    Backup Plan: Implement color-threshold filtering in software and test primarily under controlled indoor lighting.
  • Risk 2 (Hardware Delay): Shipping delays for microcontrollers/sensors.
    Backup Plan: Build a fully functional software/web simulation fallback to demonstrate system logic on schedule.
